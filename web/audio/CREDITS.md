# The island's own recordings

Every file here is played by `web/js/island-sfx.js` and must be free to publish: CC0 or public
domain only. Each is cut to mono at 32 kHz (Ogg Vorbis, `ffmpeg -ac 1 -ar 32000 -c:a libvorbis
-q:a 2 -map_metadata -1`); the island brings it to the level of the voice it replaces and loops it.

| File | What | Source | Licence |
|---|---|---|---|
| `tavern-chatter.ogg` | the village tavern's chatter | "Restaurant #3" - a restaurant at brunch, about 40 people - by Joseph SARDIN, <https://bigsoundbank.com/restaurant-3-s3377.html> | CC0 1.0 |
| `kraken-chatter.ogg` | the Salty Kraken's crew | "Restaurant #2" by Joseph SARDIN, <https://bigsoundbank.com/restaurant-2-s3376.html> | CC0 1.0 |

Additional sounds: Joseph SARDIN - [BigSoundBank.com](https://bigsoundbank.com) (credit asked for,
not required, by the CC0 licence).
