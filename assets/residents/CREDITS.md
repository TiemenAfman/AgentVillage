# Residents

`residents.blend` and `web/js/residents-mesh.js` are made by `scripts/build-residents.py` from the
same CC0 Quaternius sources as the Wanderer (bodies, hairstyles, the peasant outfit) - see
[../bodies/CREDITS.md](../bodies/CREDITS.md). The skirt, dress skirt, vest, apron, scarf, clogs, eyes
and brows are modelled by the script itself; the hats are the island's own (`web/js/villager-mesh.js`).
