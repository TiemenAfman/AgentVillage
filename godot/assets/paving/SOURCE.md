# paving138

PavingStones138 from [ambientCG](https://ambientcg.com/view?id=PavingStones138), 1K JPG.

**Licence: CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain).** No attribution required; this note is here so
the next person does not have to go looking for the answer.

Three of the maps from the pack are kept — colour, normal (OpenGL convention, which is what
Godot wants) and roughness. Ambient occlusion and displacement are not used by a
StandardMaterial3D road and were dropped rather than carried.

The two terrain textures in `../terrain/` come from the same place, by way of Terrain3D's demo.
